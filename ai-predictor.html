The models

Two models, one smarter answer

Random Forest

Builds many decision trees on random subsets of the data and averages them — robust against noise and overfitting.

XGBoost

Trains trees sequentially, each one fixing the mistakes of the last — known for high accuracy on tabular data.

Ensemble learning

Combining both balances their strengths, giving more consistent predictions than either model alone.

FAQ

Questions about the predictor

Is the AI predictor free to use?

Yes — you can try the live predictor for free, no sign-up required.

How accurate are the predictions?

Predictions are estimates based on patterns in historical data. They’re best used to compare ideas and plan content, not as guaranteed numbers. We continuously work on improving model accuracy.

Why does the app take a few seconds to load?

The predictor is hosted on a cloud platform that may “sleep” when idle. The first load can take a little longer while the server wakes up.

Can I get the predictor on my own website?

Yes — AI content insights can be included with our Creator Pro and Brand plans. Contact us to learn more.
